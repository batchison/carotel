    Section Music Technology and Production: Sound Collage

    This is a sample assignment for students enrolled in an introductory music technology and production course.
    🔗

    Subsection Introduction

    In this assignment, you will create a 1 to 2 minute composition given the requirements below.
    🔗
    Note that several aspects of this assignment have accompanying tutorials and resources that should be provided by your instructor.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection Sound Sources

    • All sound sources should be from field recordings you have made.
      🔗
      🔗
    • No recording of instruments you play, or pre-existing music (off- or online).
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection Compositional Elements

    Your piece must include all of the following:
    • Gestures/Phrases
      🔗
      🔗
    • Pad/Drone
      🔗
      🔗
    • Non-metrical loops/repetitions
      🔗
      🔗
    • A clear Beginning—Middle—Climax—End structure
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection Textural and Timbral Elements

    The piece should include the following mixing and textural components, applied at your discretion:
    • EQ
      🔗
      🔗
    • Panning
      🔗
      🔗
    • Reverb
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    No Clipping: Your rendered audio must not clip. Using proper gain-staging will help prevent this.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection Submission Requirements

    Submit a zipped (compressed) file named LASTNAME_Project 1 containing the following:
    • Your REAPER session file(s) in the .RPP format
      🔗
      🔗
    • All audio files used (.wav, .aiff, .mp4, etc.)
      🔗
      🔗
    • A losslessly rendered audio file (with a title) that can be played outside of REAPER
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection Grading

    • 10 pts — Technical competence
      🔗
      🔗
    • 10 pts — Creativity
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection NACE Competencies

    The National Association of Colleges and Employers have identified eight core competencies that help individuals to develop career readiness. This assignment will give students an opportunity to focus on three of these competencies in particular.
    🔗
    • Communication: Clearly and effectively exchange information, ideas, facts, and perspectives with persons inside and outside of an organization.
      🔗
      🔗
    • Professionalism: Knowing work environments differ greatly, understand and demonstrate effective work habits, and act in the interest of the larger community and workplace.
      🔗
      🔗
    • Technology: Understand and leverage technologies ethically to enhance efficiencies, complete tasks, and accomplish goals.
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
